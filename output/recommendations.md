## Rules

- **Assortment: Relist SKU.** An inactive SKU that sells under 1% of its market, in a market of at least €25,000 growing +20% or more.
- **Assortment: Under-performing cell.** A cell whose share of market revenue is below 60% of the category share (6.3%), in a market of at least €25,000.
- **Availability: Restock.** An active SKU that is out of stock, or on low stock in a market growing +20% or more.
- **Assortment: Assortment gap cell.** No customer SKU and mean competitor score ≥50 and mean trend ≥15% (top-right quadrant). Nearest three represented cells by normalized score/trend distance supply the observed revenue range; peer IDs are retained.
- **Products: Quadrant decision rules.** Market unit demand splits at the median; fair-share index splits at 1. Active available bottom-right SKUs are under-pushed; active bottom-left SKUs are low performers requiring viability review.
- **Price: Market unit-price extremes.** Absolute price gap ≥20% vs SKU market revenue/units; Large gap labels gaps ≥40%, indicating magnitude rather than causal confidence. Exact competitor matches are context only. This realized proxy is not shelf price.
- **Price: Constant-unit scenario.** Move current price toward SKU market unit-price proxy holding units and unit-cost proxy constant. Positive revenue uplifts receive 50–100% ranges; signed price-cut impacts and target margins remain separate. No elasticity or cost forecast is available.

## Size-of-prize bases

- Q1: Missing cells: min–max 12-week revenue of the three nearest represented competitor-signal cells (peer IDs in gap rows); sparse analogues, not forecast. Low: relist SKUs reach the 25th-percentile SKU share (3.8%) of their market. High: relist SKUs reach the median SKU share (5.1%) and under-performing cells lift to 3.8% share (60% of category share 6.3%), excluding SKUs counted in other recommendations.
- Q2: Low: not-pushed SKUs reach the 25th-percentile SKU share (3.8%) of their market, or keep 50% of current revenue where the channel trails the market by 30+ points (the larger). High: median SKU share (5.1%), or 100% of current revenue where sales are collapsing (the larger). Only flagged SKUs below these scenario targets receive positive prizes; the whole review list is not monetized.
- Q3: 50–100% movement toward SKU market unit-price proxy for below-proxy active SKUs, constant units. Above-proxy price cuts show signed revenue impact separately and are not positive opportunities. Cost proxy = current price × (1−reported margin); target margin holds that unit cost constant. Reported margin and current price may cover different periods; validate costs, tax, promotions and elasticity before action.
- Q4: Low: 50% of the 12-week revenue of 12 active out-of-stock SKUs. High: 100% of it plus 25% of the 12-week revenue of 11 low-stock SKUs in markets growing +20% or more.
- General: 12-week scenarios, not forecasts. Individual question ranges overlap. The combined total uses the maximum revenue range per SKU across actions, plus missing-cell peer ranges and residual cell opportunities; no gross profit is added.

## Caveats
