## Rules

- **Assortment: Assortment gap cell.** A subcategory × shade cell with at least one hot competitor product (signal score ≥ 50 and trend ≥ +15%) and at most 1 active in-stock SKU of our own.
- **Assortment: Relist SKU.** An inactive SKU that sells under 1% of its market, in a market of at least €25,000 growing +20% or more.
- **Assortment: Under-performing cell.** A cell whose share of market revenue is below 60% of the category share (6.3%), in a market of at least €25,000.
- **Products: Not pushed enough.** An active, not out-of-stock SKU in a market of at least €25,000 growing +10% or more, whose fair-share index is 0.25 or lower, or whose channel trend trails the market by 30 points or more.
- **Products: Low performing.** An active SKU with revenue below the bottom quartile (€1,306) where both channel and market trend are −15% or worse.
- **Price: Signal: identical competitor product.** Our price differs by 15% or more from the same product name at a competitor (above or below).
- **Price: Signal: market average.** Our price differs by 20% or more from the market average selling price (market revenue ÷ market units).
- **Price: Signal: losing ground.** Market trend is +10% or more and our channel trend trails it by 30 points or more (suggests price too high).
- **Price: Signal: thin margin.** Active SKU with margin below 25% and revenue in the top quartile (from €7,846); suggests price too low.
- **Price: Price tier.** Confirmed when the identical-product signal fires or at least two signals point the same way (all above or all below); otherwise Flag. Price vs the competitor cell median is context only: competitor pack sizes are unknown.
- **Availability: Restock.** An active SKU that is out of stock, or on low stock in a market growing +20% or more.

## Size-of-prize bases

- Q1: Low: relist SKUs reach the 25th-percentile SKU share (3.8%) of their market. High: relist SKUs reach the median SKU share (5.1%) and under-performing cells lift to 3.8% share (60% of category share 6.3%), excluding SKUs counted in other recommendations.
- Q2: Low: not-pushed SKUs reach the 25th-percentile SKU share (3.8%) of their market, or keep 50% of current revenue where the channel trails the market by 30+ points (the larger). High: median SKU share (5.1%), or 100% of current revenue where sales are collapsing (the larger).
- Q3: Low: 50% of the gross margin at stake. High: 100% of it. At stake = (competitor price − our price) × units for identical products priced 15%+ below a competitor, plus (median margin 38.8% − margin) × revenue for thin-margin big sellers.
- Q4: Low: 50% of the 12-week revenue of 12 active out-of-stock SKUs. High: 100% of it plus 25% of the 12-week revenue of 11 low-stock SKUs in markets growing +20% or more.
- General: Prizes are illustrative scenarios for 12 weeks, not forecasts. The four ranges overlap partly and are not additive.
