How SKUs are flagged

  • Fix availability: active and out of stock; or active, low stock and market trend ≥ +20%.
  • Promote / fix execution: active, not out of stock, market ≥ €25,000 and growing ≥ +10%, and either channel share < 1% or channel trend trails market trend by ≥ 30 points.
  • Price review: exact product match at a competitor with a price gap ≥ 15%; or active, margin < 25% and top-quartile revenue (≥ €7,903).
  • Relist / assortment gap: inactive, channel share < 1%, market ≥ €25,000 and growing ≥ +20%.
  • Delist / mark down: active, bottom-quartile revenue (< €1,257), channel and market trend both ≤ -15%.
  • Status check: inactive but revenue ≥ €1,257.
  • Upside at median share: market revenue × median SKU share (5.2%) − current revenue. Illustrative, not a forecast.
